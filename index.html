Standards & Sourcing

The Foundation of Our Kitchen

We do not use pre-mixed batters, soda, or commercial leavening agents. Every batch of batter undergoes a minimum 14-hour natural fermentation cycle calibrated for Latur's local climate.

  • Cast-Iron Griddles: Dosas are roasted solely on seasoned iron tavas for distinct crispness.
  • Stone-Ground Dal: Urad and raw rice are processed daily in heavy stone wet grinders to preserve grain texture.
  • Brass Decoction: Filter coffee is extracted slowly drop by drop using pure plantation beans.
