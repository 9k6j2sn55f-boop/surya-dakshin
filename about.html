Kitchen Principles

The Craft Behind Our Counter

01. 03:30 AM First Steamer Service

We open before dawn to serve commuters, students, and morning travelers. Sambar and dual chutneys are freshly ground and tempered right from the first batch.

02. Stone-Ground Batter Milling

Urad dal and raw rice are milled exclusively in heavy traditional stone wet grinders. This avoids heat generation from high-speed blades and keeps our idlis light and airy.

03. Zero Artificial Soda Pure

Every batch undergoes a natural 14-hour fermentation cycle calibrated for Latur's dry climate. We never add baking soda or chemical leaveners to artificially swell batter.

04. Cast-Iron Roasting Craft

Dosas are roasted strictly on seasoned cast-iron flat griddles over uniform heat, delivering an authentic golden crunch without synthetic non-stick coatings.

05. Single-Source Desi Ghee Ghee

From Thatte Idli podi tempering to our Karam Dosa roasts, we use single-source pure desi ghee for genuine traditional aroma and nutrition.

06. Kumbakonam Decoction Brew

First-decoction coffee brewed with single-estate roasted Arabica and chicory beans extracted drop by drop and served frothing hot in traditional brass davarahs.
